Step-by-Step Instructions

How to Install on iPhone

Choose your preferred sideloading utility. No paid Apple Developer account is required.

1

Download and Install Sideloadly on Windows

Download the free Sideloadly utility for Windows 11 from sideloadly.io. Make sure you also have iTunes and iCloud for Windows installed directly from Apple (not from Microsoft Store).

2

Connect iPhone via USB

Connect your iPhone to your Windows PC with a lightning or USB-C cable. Unlock your iPhone and tap "Trust This Computer" when prompted.

3

Drag Myspoti.ipa into Sideloadly

Drag the downloaded Myspoti.ipa file directly onto the IPA icon in Sideloadly. In the Apple ID field, enter your personal Apple ID email.

4

Click Start to Sign and Install

Click Start. Sideloadly connects directly to Apple's free developer provisioning service, signs the app with your personal certificate, and installs it onto your iPhone.

5

Trust Developer Certificate on iPhone

On your iPhone, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Under Developer App, select your Apple ID and tap Trust. If on iOS 16 or 17+, make sure Developer Mode is turned ON in Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode.

1

Install AltServer on Windows

Download and install AltServer on Windows. Ensure iTunes and iCloud (downloaded from Apple directly) are running.

2

Install AltStore onto iPhone

Connect iPhone via cable, click the AltServer tray icon in Windows, and choose Install AltStore > [Your iPhone] using your Apple ID.

3

Transfer Myspoti.ipa to iPhone

Download Myspoti.ipa on your iPhone using Safari, or transfer it to the Files app via iCloud Drive or AirDrop.

4

Sideload via AltStore

Open AltStore on your iPhone, go to the My Apps tab, tap the + icon in the top left, and select Myspoti.ipa to install.

1

Set up SideStore with WireGuard Pairing

Follow the SideStore setup guide to pair your device once via PC using the local WireGuard VPN profile.

2

Download Myspoti.ipa in Mobile Safari

Open this webpage on your iPhone and tap Download Latest IPA to save Myspoti.ipa directly to your local Files app.

3

Install and Refresh Completely Computer-Free

Open SideStore, enable the local WireGuard VPN toggle, tap +, and pick Myspoti.ipa. SideStore signs and refreshes the app directly over Wi-Fi without ever needing a computer again.

Important Signing & Expiration Notice

Apple imposes a 7-day expiration policy on apps signed with free personal Apple accounts (non-paid developer tiers). After 7 days, the app simply needs to be refreshed (via Sideloadly in 10 seconds or automatically in the background using SideStore/AltStore). Never share your Apple ID password with any third-party website. This distribution website never asks for, handles, or stores credentials of any kind.
